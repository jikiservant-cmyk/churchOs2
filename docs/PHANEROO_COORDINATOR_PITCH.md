# Getting the Central campuses coordinator — the whole play

*You have people inside the fellowships. That is the only asset that matters here. Use it for an **introduction**,
not a campaign.*

**Send with it:** `docs/ChurchOS_Manifest_Campus_One_Pager.pdf` (one page, phone-readable)

---

## 1. First, make sure you have the *right* person

Manifest has several kinds of coordinator. Ask for the exact one:

| Title you want | What they control | Ask for it like this |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ **Central campuses coordinator** (a.k.a. Manifest Central regional coordinator) | The campus fellowships in the Central region — the report forms, the leaders' meetings | *"Who receives the campus attendance reports for Central?"* |
| ❌ Centre coordinator | Manifest **centres** (livestream rooms), not campuses | Different person, different budget |
| ❌ Fellowship chair (Makerere) | One campus only | Your proof site, not the buyer |
| ❌ "The Apostle's office" | Everything, and therefore nothing you can reach | Never your opening |

**The question that gets you the right name:**
> "Who receives the campus attendance reports for the Central region?"

That is precise, it names a real job, and it cannot be answered with "just come to the main service."

---

## 2. The message to your friend inside the fellowship

Keep it small enough that they can say yes in one reply.

> "Bro quick one — who receives the campus attendance reports for Manifest Central? I need a name and a
> number. I've built a free attendance + follow-up system for campus fellowships and I want to show them for
> 10 minutes. Nothing for sale, no cost to the ministry."

**If they offer to "push it for you,"** say: *"Don't push anything — just put me in the group chat or send the
number. I'll do the talking."* Never spend a friend's credibility on your pitch.

**If they don't know the name:** ask them to forward one question to the campus WhatsApp group — *"who is our
Central campuses coordinator?"* Somebody there will answer in five minutes.

---

## 3. The message to the coordinator (WhatsApp)

Send the one-pager as a PDF **in the same message**, then this text:

> "Good evening [name], I'm [name] from Kampala — a developer. I've built a free attendance and records system
> for campus fellowships: each campus takes attendance with one passkey, and the region gets one dashboard
> instead of report forms.
>
> No cost to the ministry, nothing to buy, and no money passes through it. I've attached one page.
>
> Could I have 10 minutes — on a call, or at the next campuses leaders' meeting? I'll set up one campus
> myself in under ten minutes so you can see it working this week."

**Then stop.** Do not send a second message the same day.

---

## 4. The 10-minute call, minute by minute

| Min | What you do |
|---|---|
| 0–1 | One sentence: *"A dashboard instead of report forms — free."* Then be quiet |
| 1–4 | **Ask, don't pitch:** "How do the campus numbers reach you today? Who fills the form? How late do they arrive? Which campuses have gone quiet and how did you find out?" Let them complain |
| 4–8 | **Demo live:** create their first campus on the call, take attendance with a passkey, open the dashboard with two campuses in it. Phone, not laptop |
| 8–10 | **The ask:** a slot at the next leaders' meeting, and the names of the campuses reporting to them |

**The line that closes it:**
> "Give me the next leaders' meeting. Not a partnership — fifteen minutes in front of the leaders, and they can
> each take it or leave it."

---

## 5. What to ask for — and what never to ask for

**Ask for:**
1. **Fifteen minutes at the next Central campuses leaders' meeting** — the single highest-value thing they own.
2. **The list of campus leaders reporting to them** (or the WhatsApp group) — distribution.
3. **One sentence of endorsement** — "use this for your reports" — no logo, no branding change.

**Never ask for:**
- ❌ Money. Not from a coordinator; the campus arm has no budget.
- ❌ A meeting with Apostle Lubega. Wrong door, wrong month.
- ❌ Exclusivity, a logo on their site, or an "official partner" announcement. Brand-protected ministry, and
  you gain nothing.

---

## 6. What happens if it works

The coordinator is worth more as a **route** than as a customer:

```
Coordinator says yes
      → 15 minutes at the Central leaders' meeting
      → 20-40 campus leaders hear it from their own coordinator, not from you
      → those leaders are also members of churches across Kampala
      → the ministry's own centres-administration conversation opens later
```

**The second-order value is the real one:** every campus leader you meet is a young person with a home church,
and a home church is a 17,000 activation. Never sell to the student — but always ask, at the end, *"which
church do you fellowship at when you're not on campus?"* That question is worth more than the fellowship
account.
