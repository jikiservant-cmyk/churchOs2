# Fellowships of *the Expanse type*

**Question:** find more fellowships like **The Worship Expanse**.

**Discipline:** this list only includes things that have the *same shape* as Expanse — an evening
worship fellowship that hires a venue and pulls a young crowd, not a church with a youth service.
Churches that merely run a youth programme are in the reject pile at the bottom.

---

## 1. What "the Expanse type" actually is

Five traits. A movement has to have at least four to be on this list.

| # | Trait | Expanse |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Worship is the main event**, not the warm-up to a sermon | Yes — "a refilling sector of the Holy Spirit" |
| 2 | **Hires or borrows a venue** — school, hall, hotel road, another church's building | Grace Pentecostal Church Nansana, Hoima Rd |
| 3 | **Evening, weekly or monthly**, 2–3 hours, not a Sunday service | 6:30 pm |
| 4 | **Young crowd** — students, campus, 20s | Yes |
| 5 | **Runs on socials**, not on a website or a signboard | TikTok `@worship.expanse` |

Why the shape matters for churchOs: this is a **room that fills and empties every week** with no
membership roll. Nobody knows who came back. That's the pain, and it's a pain the leader already
feels every single week.

## 5. What the pitch is for this layer

Never "church management system". The room isn't a church.

> **"You fill this room every Friday. Who came back next Friday?"**

Then the three lists they all keep badly:

1. **Who came** — the room fills, and there is no roll
2. **Who is in the cohort** — worship schools, training intakes, altar teams
3. **Who is still serving** — the team turns over every term and nobody notices until the night

For The Worship House specifically, the sharper version:

> **"Three Prayer Waves, three school playgrounds, one Friday-night crowd each. Are the three
> lists on the same sheet, or are they three WhatsApp groups?"**
