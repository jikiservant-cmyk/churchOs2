## 2. What all of them have in common — and why it's your opening

Every one of these movements keeps **three lists, badly**:

1. **Who came to the gathering.** Worship nights fill rooms; nobody knows who was there twice, and nobody can
   follow up the person who came forward.
2. **Who is in the training cohort.** TWG trains worship leaders by intake. That is a class list, an alumni
   list and a "who finished" list — all on paper or WhatsApp.
3. **Who serves on the team.** Singers, instrumentalists, media, ushers, intercessors. Teams turn over every
   term, and the roster lives in somebody's phone.

**So the pitch is never "church management". It is:**

> "You fill rooms and train people. How do you know who came back, who finished the training, and who is
> still serving?"

That question is unanswerable for most of them — which is why it works.

---

## 3. The pitch, by type

**For a worship fellowship (Expanse, Remnants, Koinonia):**
> "Attendance per gathering, and the first-timers captured with a phone number, then three follow-up SMS.
> Your worship nights stop being a crowd and start being a list you can call."

**For a training ministry (TWG):**
> "Every intake is a class: register, attendance per session, completion, and an alumni list you can SMS when
> the next summit opens. Your graduates become a network instead of a memory."

**For a church with fellowships inside it (KBC, Lugogo Baptist, KTC, UCF):**
> "Every fellowship gets its own list and its own attendance; the pastor sees all of them in one view. Home
> cells, young adults, professionals, youth — one dashboard."

## 4. The money rule for this whole layer

**A fellowship is almost never the buyer. The mother church is.**

| Fellowship | Who actually has a budget |
|---|---|
| Worship Expanse | **Grace Pentecostal Church, Nansana** |
| Koinonia | **Christ's Heart Ministry International, Mukono** |
| TWG | ROLEC Makerere (host), or TWG's own partner funding — treat as free/low |
| UCF | **UCF itself** |
| KBC / Lugogo Baptist / KTC | the church |

So: **use the fellowship to get the introduction, and close with the church that hosts it.** Same two-step as
everywhere else — proof at the bottom, decision at the top. 17,000 once, per church.

## 6. Honest cautions

- **Worship movements are donor-funded and proud of it.** Expect "we don't charge people." Fine — you are not
  charging the fellowship; the host church is the customer.
- **Verify before you travel.** Remnants' meeting day and venue rest on a single 2021 travel blog. Koinonia's
  "Bativa" venue is from 2015 reporting. Confirm on social media before spending a fare.
- **Do not confuse similar names.** Remnants Haven Ministries (Pr. Dickson Lubinga) is a different
  organisation from The Remnants Fellowship; "Remnant Light Ministry International" is a third. Confirm the
  venue, not the word.
- **Music ministries gossip across each other fast.** One bad experience with a worship team closes several
  doors at once. Do the first one properly, free, and let them talk.
