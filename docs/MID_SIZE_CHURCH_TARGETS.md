## How to get in the door (ranked by success rate)

1. **Referral from a pastor already on the app.** Ask each pastor who activates: "Who is your
   overseer / who ordained you?" Warm intros to a bishop convert at maybe 10× cold calls.
2. **Sunday service → church administrator.** Attend second service, ask the admin (not the bishop)
   for 15 minutes on Tuesday. Admins are the ones who feel the pain of member records.
3. **The bishop's mobile, WhatsApp first.** All the numbers above are direct mobiles. Message, don't call:
   *"Kabona, good morning. I built a church system used by [X] churches — members, attendance, SMS to
   your branches. May I show you 10 minutes on Tuesday? I'll come to Makindye."*
4. **BAF / NFBPC district bishop.** A district bishop with 20–30 churches can convene them for you.
   BAF secretariat: +256 773 089 129 · NFBPC: nfbpc.secretariat@gmail.com, Bible House, Bombo Rd.
5. **Directory mining** (do this tonight, 2 hours):
   - `bornagainfaith.org/listings` — member churches with contacts
   - `paguganda.org` — **each pastorate page lists its bishop and its church count**
   - `churchguideuganda.com/churches?district=Kampala`
   - Google Maps: search "church" in Makindye, Nansana, Kira, Mukono — look for names containing "Ministries", "Centre", "Fellowship"; those are the multi-branch kind.

## The 3-minute pitch for a 30-branch pastor

> "Bishop, right now when you want to reach all your branches you call 30 pastors one by one, and
> when you want the attendance of the whole ministry you wait for a paper report. This system gives
> you one dashboard for every branch — members, attendance, converts — and lets you SMS all your
> members from one wallet at 50 shillings a message, cheaper than what you pay now.
>
> My normal price is 17,000 per branch, once. Because you're bringing 30, I'll do the whole ministry
> and throw in 1,000 free SMS so you can announce to everyone this Sunday. Your pastors register with
> one code I give you, and each one activates from his own phone — you don't chase anybody."

**The mechanism that makes "each church activates on their own" true:** you mint **one denomination
invite code** (up to 1000 uses, up to 90 days) and hand it over. Branches self-provision with it, then
each pays their own 17,000 from their own MoMo. That is exactly the model you described — and the app
already does it via `overseer_create_invite` → redeem at signup or Settings → Denomination Network.

⚠️ **Prep before the meeting:** denominations are created **SQL-side** (no UI — see
`docs/DENOMINATIONS.md` known issue #2). So create the denomination and mint the invite code
*before* you sit with the bishop, or you'll be fumbling in front of him.

## Objection handling

| He says | You say |
|---|---|
| "We already use WhatsApp groups" | "WhatsApp can't tell you who attended, who's new, or who stopped coming. And it can't count your members for a diocesan report." |
| "17,000 is too much per branch" | "It's once, not monthly. One funeral announcement SMS to 300 members costs 15,000 — this replaces that forever. And 1,000 free SMS is included." |
| "My pastors have no money" | "Start with 5 branches at 85,000. If the dashboard doesn't help you in 30 days, don't bring the other 25." |
| "Let me think about it" | "Fair. Let me register your HQ branch right now, free for 7 days, and you judge it on your own attendance this Sunday." |
| "Who else uses it?" | Name every church already live. If that number is small, say "you'd be among the first 100 churches — that's why you're getting the price." Never inflate it; pastors talk to each other. |
