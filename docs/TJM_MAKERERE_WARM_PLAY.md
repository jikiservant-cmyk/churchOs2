# TJM Makerere — the warm play

*You have a connection inside TJM Makerere. That means the proposal is no longer the opener — it's the
supporting document on the pastor's table.*

---

## The rule for a warm connection

**Ask your contact for an introduction, not an endorsement.** Never ask a friend to champion your product.
If it goes well, fine; if it goes badly, you have spent their credibility and your friendship. One ask:
*"Who should I talk to, and will you introduce me?"*

**Do not send the PDF first.** A 2-page proposal arriving cold on WhatsApp reads like a vendor. The PDF is
for the table, after a face-to-face. Send nothing but words to your friend.

---

## 0. No pilots. No three Sundays.

A "pilot with a review after three Sundays" is for someone with a month to spare. You have until Friday. So
the shape changes:

| Pilot thinking | What you actually do |
|---|---|
| Free trial for 3 weeks | **Live in the room the same day** — names imported, passkeys issued, first SMS sent before you leave |
| Proof after 3 Sundays | **Proof on Wednesday night** — Oikos Time is 6pm, two days from now. Watch a passkey take attendance and photograph the count |
| Review meeting later | **Review at Oikos Time tonight.** "It worked, it took 8 minutes" — that is the review |
| Rollout next month | **Two introductions on Thursday.** Mengo and one more, set up by phone at 17,000 each on the day |

**The rule:** you never leave a room without the system live, and you never wait a week for a number you can
have tonight.

## 1. The message to your contact

Keep it short, low-pressure, and specific. Copy this:

> "Bro, quick one. I've built a system for churches — new converts with phone numbers, oikos attendance, and
> SMS follow-up. I want to give it **free to TJM Makerere** as a pilot and let the results speak for
> themselves.
>
> Can you introduce me to the **location pastor**, or tell me who handles **follow-up** there? I don't need a
> big meeting — 20 minutes after a Sunday service is enough."

**Why this wording works:** free · pilot · Makerere only · 20 minutes · after service. Nothing to fear, no
budget conversation, no commitment asked of anyone.

**If they ask "what's the catch?":**
> "No catch for Makerere — free for 12 months. If it works, the other locations pick it up and each one pays
> 17,000 once. That's the whole business model."

## 2. Who your connection is decides your path

| Your connection | What to ask them for | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Just a member / student** | An intro to their **oikos leader** first, not the pastor | Lowest friction. The oikos leader becomes your internal witness |
| **An oikos leader** | Permission to onboard **their oikos this Wednesday, free** | They feel the attendance pain personally. One working oikos is the proof the pastor needs |
| **Media / streaming team** | A conversation about how the **Iganga campaign cards** were handled | The media team usually ends up owning follow-up by default — and they're the ones who'll understand the SMS pipeline immediately |
| **Staff or a pastor** | A **20-minute slot** in their week | Skip the theatre, go straight to the decision |

**Best path if you can choose it:** member → oikos leader → location pastor. Each step is a small yes, and
by the time you reach the pastor you are not a stranger with a PDF, you are "the guy [name] brought."

## 3. The 20-minute meeting — Makerere version, not the CGC version

At Makerere you are not selling a movement-wide rollout. You are selling **one location, one oikos, three
Sundays.**

| Min | What you do |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | One sentence: *"I built the follow-up side of a campaign — names, three SMS, oikos attendance."* Then stop talking |
| 2–7 | **Ask, don't pitch.** "After the Iganga campaign, how did you follow up the converts here? Where do the names go? How do you take oikos attendance today?" Let them describe the mess |
| 7–12 | **Demo on their table.** Import 300 names from a CSV on your phone. Show oikos check-in with one passkey. Show the three SMS templates |
| 12–16 | **Do it now, not later.** "Let's load your oikos list right now — it takes eight minutes." Set it up in the meeting. Not a follow-up email: in the room |
| 16–19 | **The offer.** Free for Makerere, 12 months, because they are the reference site. Every other location is 17,000 on the day it is set up. Ask for **Mengo and one more, tomorrow** |
| 19–20 | **Fix tomorrow.** "I'll come to Oikos Time at 6pm and watch the attendance come in." A time and a place, tonight, or it does not happen |

## 4. Why *now* is the perfect week at Makerere — use these three

**1. December is coming and students are leaving.**
A campus congregation in mid-November is about to lose half its people to upcountry travel for the holidays.
Without phone numbers, they are gone until February. With a list and SMS, you re-gather them in January.
**This is the single strongest argument you have this month:** *"What is your plan to reach these students
when they go home in December?"*

**2. The list outlives the committee.**
Campus leadership turns over every year — new exec, new oikos leaders, and the paper register walks out with
the old secretary. A system means the location keeps its people when its leaders change.

**3. Exam season is when attendance quietly collapses.**
Every location sees empty seats in November and calls it "exams." The ones who stop coming are the ones who
never come back. Three SMS to absentees is the cheapest pastoral care in the building.

**TJM alone cannot give you 1.5M by Friday.** With 32 branches it can give you **480,000–527,000** — but only
if leadership decides in the next few days, and only if you get the meeting. Everybody below leadership can
give you a warm yes and nothing else.

So: the Makerere visit is your proof, the leadership meeting is your money, and both have to happen this week.

What TJM gives you, and it is worth the day:

1. **Speed** — a live location in one meeting, proof in eight hours.
2. **A reference with a face and a video** — the thing that shortens every bishop and network call after it.
3. **2–4 activations this week** if Thursday goes well (68,000 at most).

So you run two tracks in parallel, and you do not confuse them:

TJM is your proof. The networks are your money. Do TJM in the morning and make your calls in the afternoon —
and use the Makerere story in every one of those calls.
