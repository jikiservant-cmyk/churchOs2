# The pastors'-kid network

**What it is:** a room full of people whose fathers lead churches, who are (almost always) the
unpaid ones already doing the attendance sheet, the media desk and the youth programme.

**Why it is the best asset disclosed so far:** every other lane in this file needs a gatekeeper
opened — a coordinator, a branch list, a secretary. A PK *is* the gatekeeper's child. There is no
door to knock on. And because he is usually the one who would have to run the thing anyway, he is
also the implementation labour that every other lane is missing.

---

## 1. The three rules for this channel

**Rule 1 — Never sell to the PK. Always sell to the father.**
The PK's job is one sentence: *"Dad, my friend built this, can he show you?"* The moment you hand a
friend a price list and a target, you have recruited him into your salesforce, and he will feel it
by the second ask. He opens the door; you walk through it and do the selling.

**Rule 2 — Never mention the fee pressure in that group.**
They already know what church money looks like from the inside. A PK who hears "I need this by
Friday" will want to help you — and helping you is not the same as getting his father to pay. It
also travels: one PK tells another, and the whole group becomes a favour instead of a product.

**Rule 3 — Ask a question before you ask for anything.**
The group's real value in week one is language, not money. Ask them how their fathers count
attendance and you get, for free, the exact words to use in every other call this week — and a
dozen replies from people who now expect to hear from you again.

---

## 2. The arithmetic

| PKs who say yes | Branches | UGX |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 5 | 85,000 |
| 10 | 10 | 170,000 |
| 20 | 20 | 340,000 |

Small money on its own — so do not treat this as the Friday lane. Treat it as:

1. **Proof sites** that cost you nothing to acquire and that you can visit in person.
2. **A reference list** — "the branch at [church], the pastor is [name], call him". That sentence is
   what closes the next cold call.
3. **Language** for the pitches in the rest of the playbook.
4. **The compounding bit:** PKs know PKs from other churches, camps and Bible college. One good
   introduction per PK is where the real number is.

Ask for the **introduction**, not the sale. Ten introductions are worth more than ten activations.

---

## 3. Move 1 — tonight: the question, not the pitch

Post this in the group. It asks for nothing and gives you everything.

> Quick question for the PKs whose dads are still doing attendance on paper 👇
>
> When your dad asks "so how many came on Sunday?" — what actually happens? Is there a book, an
> Excel sheet, or does everybody just start guessing?
>
> I'm building something small for this (one dashboard, the roll, and the report that goes up to the
> district) and I want to get the question right before I build more of it. Tell me what your church
> actually does. 🙏

**Why this works and a pitch doesn't:** it is true, it is useful to them, it produces replies, and
nobody has to defend their father's church. Every reply is a warm thread you can take to a one-to-one
tomorrow.

---

## 4. Move 2 — tomorrow: the one-to-one

Only to the ones who replied. Never the group again.

> Bro, thanks for that. Since you're the one who ends up doing it anyway 😅 — I've built the thing.
> It's one dashboard: the roll, who came, who's new, and the report. Runs from a phone, no app to
> download, and each branch sets itself up, so you don't have to babysit it.
>
> 17,000 per branch, one time — not a subscription.
>
> Two things, honestly:
> 1. If your dad's branch would use it, give me his name and the branch, and **I'll come and set it
>    up myself**. You don't have to do anything.
> 2. If you know another PK whose dad leads a church, that introduction is worth more to me than
>    anything else this week.
>
> And if it's not for your dad's church, no stress — tell me what's wrong with it instead. That's
> just as useful.

Note what that message does: it asks for **his father's name**, not his father's money; it removes
all work from him; and it gives him a dignified way to say no.

---

## 5. Move 3 — Friday: the branch list becomes phone calls

By Friday you should have a handful of names. Then run it exactly like every other lane:

1. Call the father. Open with the PK: *"Good morning pastor — I'm [name], I'm a friend of your son
   [X]. He said this might be useful to you."* That one sentence replaces your entire credibility
   problem.
2. Ask the roll question: *"How do you know today who came last week?"*
3. Price per branch, 17,000, once. Deadline framing — branches travel home in December.
4. Ask which other branches he is connected to. A PK's father is rarely pastoring one church alone.

---

## 6. If the group turns out to be organised

If it is not a WhatsApp group but a **fellowship with leaders, chapters or a patron**, then you are
not talking to peers — you are talking to a body, and the play changes:

- **The body is a door, not a buyer.** Do not try to sell the fellowship a system.
- Ask for **one thing**: fifteen minutes at the next meeting, or a slot to teach one session on
  "running the church's records when you have no office".
- The chapter leader is your version of the Manifest coordinator: the person who can put you in front
  of thirty pastors' children in one evening.
- The follow-on is the same as Manifest: prove in the room, invoice the churches behind each PK.

---

## 7. What not to do

- **Don't broadcast a flyer** to the group. A flyer in a friends' group reads as hustle.
- **Don't ask a PK to convince his father.** Ask for the name; let the father decide on his own phone.
- **Don't discount for friends** unless the person is a chapter leader who can introduce you to many
  churches — and even then, give **labour** (you set it up) rather than a lower price. A discounted
  price in a friends' group becomes the price.
- **Don't chase the ones who say nothing.** The group will have PKs who are estranged from ministry,
  or whose fathers have died, or who simply left. Silence is an answer; leave it.
- **Don't let this replace the phone calls.** PK replies arrive in the evening; the Worship House and
  TJM calls happen in the morning.
