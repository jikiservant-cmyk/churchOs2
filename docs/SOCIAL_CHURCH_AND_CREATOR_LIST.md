# Youth-Heavy Churches, Artists and Creators in Uganda — social reach list

Collected 2026-11-17 from public profiles and influencer-ranking aggregators (Scrumball, Modash, Hafi,
Watchdog Uganda's TikTok 2025 report). **Follower counts move weekly and aggregator numbers can lag —
treat them as order-of-magnitude, verify on the profile before you pitch.** Handles below are the ones
I could confirm; where I could not confirm a handle I say so rather than inventing one.

---

## ⚠️ Read this before you pay anybody

**Follower count is not reach.** Check these three things on the profile itself:

1. **Engagement rate.** A 245K account at **0.2%** engagement is worth less than a 30K account at 9%.
   Several big Ugandan gospel accounts sit under 1%.
2. **Comment language.** Real Ugandan church audiences comment in **Luganda** ("Amina", "Kale", "Weebale").
   If the comments are emoji-only or foreign-language spam, the audience isn't yours.
3. **Posting cadence.** No post in 30 days = no audience to sell to, whatever the number says.

**Never pay upfront for a post.** Pay per activated church, same day, exactly like the youth-leader
commission. If a creator insists on cash before posting, walk away — there are fifty more.

## Tier 1 — Youth-heavy churches and ministries (the real prize)

These are organisations, not individuals: one conversation can mean many churches.

### 🥇 Phaneroo Ministries International — the single best fit in Uganda
- **Why:** founded 2014 by Apostle Grace Lubega; the congregation is **predominantly youth**, and its
  teachings are explicitly built for TikTok, Instagram and WhatsApp distribution. It runs **600+
  "manifest centres"** across the country that livestream services.
- **That is 600 distributed congregations** — structurally identical to the diocese tier your app
  already supports (one overseer, many centres, one dashboard).
- **Reach:** services at UMA Showgrounds, Lugogo; claims 500,000 weekly in person and virtual.
- **Approach:** don't chase Apostle Lubega. **Ask for the office that manages manifest centres**, then
  pitch the centre-leader dashboard. Handles: ministry accounts on Facebook, Instagram, X;
  @apostlegracelubega on Instagram (his personal teaching account).
- Sources: Monitor, 10 Aug 2024 · kampalaedgetimes.com

### Light the World Church — Nansana (Ps. Wilson Bugembe)
- Youth-magnet church (sanctuary rebuilt for ~6,000) and Bugembe is also a major gospel artist with a
  young, celebrity-adjacent following. Perfect profile: **one pastor, one decision, youth-heavy.**
- Approach: the church office in Nansana; he is publicly reachable and does collaborations.

### Watoto Church — 17–18 campuses, ~30,000 weekly
- Cell-based, youth-strong, and highly centralised (one ops decision covers every campus).

### House of Prayer Ministries International (Ps. Aloysius Bugingo)

- Approach: media/ministry office; the media arm is the more useful conversation.

### Kampala Bible Revelation Church — Makerere

## DM scripts (short, no pitch, no price)

**To a creator:**
> "Bro, I'm a Ugandan founder. I built a church system that keeps members, tracks attendance and sends
> SMS at 50/=. I'm giving it to 30 youth leaders free this month. Would you try it at your church and, if
> you like it, post once with your code? You get 3,000 per church that signs up — paid same day."

**To a church's social admin (Phaneroo centre, Light the World, Watoto):**
> "Hi, who manages the manifest centres / campuses? I've built a dashboard that lets a centre leader see
> his own members and attendance and report upward. I'd like to ask for a 15-minute meeting with whoever
> coordinates centres — not the main office."

**To an artist's manager:**
> "I'm not asking for a paid post. I want to give your artist a free church account and a
> per-activation commission on any church that joins through his code. No upfront cost, nothing to lose."

## Reality check

- **Phaneroo is the one that could change your year.** 600+ youth-heavy centres in a structure your app
  already models. But it is also a real organisation with gatekeepers — start the conversation this
  week, expect weeks not days.
- Creators give you **attention**, not customers. Attention converts at 0.5–2% for a product like this.
  A 2M-follower video might yield 3–10 churches. Good, but never your primary channel.
- The artists with the biggest numbers (Levixone at 0.2% engagement) are the **least** likely to move
  churches. Verify engagement before you spend a single hour.
- **Every creator, artist and church you land should be handed to a youth leader to implement.** You
  cannot onboard 30 churches yourself while running a 4-day sprint.
