## What we offer them — free, and specific to their model

**1. The convert pipeline (the headline).**
Campaign cards → bulk import → **new-convert record with the name, phone, location and who prayed with
them** → automatic **Day 1 / Day 3 / Day 7 SMS** → assignment to an oikos. A campaign stops being a number
and becomes a list of people somebody is responsible for.

**2. Oikos attendance that a cell leader can actually keep.**
One passkey per oikos, any phone, no login, no app install. The location pastor sees which oikoses are
alive and which have quietly stopped meeting.

**3. Location-level oversight.**
The founder and the national team see **all locations in one view** — members, attendance, converts,
activity — without calling seven pastors for figures. Already built and running.

**TJM Makerere is free for 12 months** — it is the reference site. **Every other location pays 17,000 UGX
once, on the day it is set up**, because each location is its own congregation and activates on its own. SMS
at **50/= per message**, from each location's own wallet.

## Honest limits — say these out loud if asked

  after the records and follow-up work.

  churches understands costs; what it cannot forgive is a surprise.
