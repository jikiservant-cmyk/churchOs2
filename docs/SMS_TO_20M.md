# 20 million a month, through SMS — the honest arithmetic

Buy 25 · sell 50 · margin 25 · minimum 5,000/week per church.

---

## 1. The direct answer

| Average per church | Churches needed for 20M/month |
|---|---|
| 5,000/week (the minimum) | **1,846** |
| 10,000/week | 923 |
| 15,000/week | 615 |
| 20,000/week | 462 |
| 30,000/week | 308 |

**At the 5,000 minimum: 1,846 churches.** For comparison, all of Phaneroo is ~670 branches, and at
5,000/week that is 7.26M/month — a third of the target, from the largest single network you have access
to.

So the sentence to accept now: **20M/month is not an SMS-at-5,000 outcome.** It is either 1,846 small
churches (2.7× all of Phaneroo) or ~600 churches spending three times the minimum. Both are 12–24 month
projects, not a semester.

---

## 2. What 20M/month actually requires

| | |
|---|---|
| Margin | 20,000,000 |
| Church money through the wallet | **40,000,000/month** |
| Messages sent | **800,000/month** |
| **Your Africa's Talking bill** | **20,000,000/month** |

That last line is the one nobody plans for: you pay AT **before** the churches pay you, and a wallet
top-up can lag a Sunday by days. At this scale you need roughly **5M of float** in the account at all
times, or messages start failing on the busiest day of the week. Cash-flow, not sales, is what breaks
this business at scale.

## 3. What the life actually costs (your numbers to correct)

Assumptions below are Kampala ballparks — **overwrite them and the church counts move directly**:

| Line | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Rent (2-bedroom, decent area) | 700,000 |
| Maintaining yourself | 700,000 |
| Car, 32M over the 5 months to next semester | 6,400,000 |
| **Total for this semester** | **7,800,000/month** |
| **Total over 5 months** | **39,000,000** |

Churches needed to fund **39M over 5 months**:

| Average per church | Churches |
|---|---|
| 5,000/week | **720** |
| 15,000/week | 240 |
| 30,000/week | 120 |

**After the car is bought**, the burn drops to 1.4M/month — which is **129 churches at the 5,000
minimum**, or 43 churches at 15,000/week.

That is the number that matters, and it is a third of 20M. Rent and a car and food do not cost 20M a
month; they cost about 1.4M a month plus a one-off 32M. **20M/month is a different goal** (savings,
staff, a second car, property) and should be treated as year two, not semester one.

## 4. The ladder you can actually climb

| Stage | Churches | Margin/month |
|---|---|---|
| Worship House + the fellowships | 12 | 130,000 |
| + TJM's 32 branches | 44 | 476,667 |
| + one mid-size network (100) | 144 | 1,560,000 |
| + a second network (250 total) | 294 | 3,185,000 |
| + Phaneroo-scale (670) | 670 | 7,258,333 |

**44 churches — TJM plus what you already have — is 476,667/month.** That covers rent and your upkeep
with room to spare, and it is roughly one network conversation plus the fellowships already in motion.

**144 churches covers rent, upkeep and the car in five months.** That is TJM + one more network of the
same size. Not 1,846 churches — one more conversation like the first one.

---

## 5. The three levers, in order of power

1. **Average spend per church, not church count.** 615 churches at 15,000/week = 20M. 1,846 at 5,000.
   The cheapest way up is to sell the church that messages weekly a *bigger* weekly amount — the 400-member
   church at 20,000/week is worth 43,333/month on its own.
2. **Networks, not churches.** One denominational conversation delivers 100–670 tenants at once. Every
   individual church is a Tuesday afternoon; every network is a month of income.
3. **The rate only at the margin.** At 50 you keep 50%. If a large network asks for 45, you keep 20 and
   the volume may make it worth it — but only network-wide, in writing, never per-church.

---

## 6. Bottom line

- **20M/month = 1,846 churches at the minimum.** Not this semester. Say it out loud so you stop
  planning against it.
- **The life = 7.8M/month for five months**, then 1.4M/month. That is **720 churches at the minimum**,
  or 240 at 15,000/week, or **144 if TJM and one more network land.**
- **The realistic semester target is 144 churches**, and the realistic prayer is one more TJM.
