# The Youth-Leader Channel

**The idea:** youth leaders are the ones who will actually type in the members, run the usher
check-in and send the SMS. Win them, and they carry the app into their church and sell it upward
to the pastor. This document is the organisation list, the ambassador terms, and the 2-hour
training that turns a room of youth leaders into a distribution army.

---

## First, the honest frame

| | Bishops | Youth leaders |
|---|---|---|
| Money per conversation | 340k–850k | 0 (they have no money) |
| Who pays | the bishop | the pastor, *after* the youth leader sells it upward |
| Speed to cash | days | 1–4 weeks per church, but compounding |
| Volume over 60 days | 2–4 deals | 30 leaders × 3 churches = **90 activations** |
| Authority risk | low | medium — a pastor who feels bypassed will block you |

**So:** youth leaders are not a replacement for this week's bishop calls. They are how you reach
89 churches in the *next* 60 days without making 89 cold calls. Run both. One tactical youth play
still fits inside this week — a Saturday training (below) — because it can produce 5–10 churches
in one afternoon.

## The Ambassador Programme

**What a youth leader gets**
- Free use of the system for his youth ministry once his pastor approves the church account.
- **3,000 UGX commission** per church that activates through his code (18% of the 17,000), paid by
  MoMo the same day. Or 1,000 free SMS credits if he'd rather have credit than cash.
- A printed certificate and a WhatsApp group with the other ambassadors. Status matters more than
  money to this group — give both.

**How you track it without writing code**
Every ambassador gets **his own invite code** (`overseer`/church invite, `max_uses` set to what he's
chasing, expiry 90 days). Every church that redeems that code is visibly his. Attribution is
automatic and you pay on the code, not on trust. No referral system needs building.

**The protocol that protects you**
1. **The pastor approves first.** Never onboard a church behind a pastor's back — that is how you get
   permanently banned from a network.
2. **The commission comes from your margin, never from church money.** Say that sentence out loud to
   the pastor so nothing looks like a kickback.
3. **The youth leader is not your salesperson.** He is your *implementer*. You close the pastor; he
   does the data entry, which is what he'd enjoy anyway.

## The 2-hour training (the whole curriculum)

Run it Saturday morning, 9–11am, in a church hall. 20–30 youth leaders. Target: **5–10 churches
activated in the room.**

| Time | Module | What they actually do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Why the pastor cares | Show `/overseer` and the pastor dashboard. 5 minutes of "this is what he sees" |
| 0:10 | **Members in 10 minutes** | `CSVUploader` takes their paper register. Bring a sample CSV — 300 members imported live, in front of them. This is the moment they decide |
| 0:35 | Usher check-in | Passkey flow: one code, no login on the door, works on any phone. Ushers love it because it removes arguments at the gate |
| 0:55 | SMS to youth | Send a real SMS to the room. Show the cost: 50 UGX each, wallet debited, delivery report back |
| 1:15 | The reports the pastor asks for | Attendance trend, new converts, visitors, who stopped coming |
| 1:35 | Onboarding | Each leader registers their church with their code, calls the pastor on speaker, gets the 17,000 approved **while sitting there** |
| 1:55 | Ambassador terms | Commission, code, WhatsApp group, certificate |

**Kit to bring:** laptop, HDMI adapter, phone hotspot, a printed sample CSV, 30 one-page price
sheets, your MoMo number, and the invite codes already minted for each leader.

⚠️ Denominations and some invite flows are created **SQL-side** (`docs/DENOMINATIONS.md`, known
issue #2). Mint every code the night before. Do not mint codes in front of 30 people.

## Risks, stated plainly

1. **Authority.** A pastor who learns his youth leader "joined a program" without him will refuse on
   principle. Always pastor-first.
2. **Side-hustle optics.** Commission paid in cash to a young person inside a church can look like
   buying influence. Keep it public, signed, and small (3,000 is defensible; 8,000 is not).
3. **Churn of the ambassador himself.** Youth leaders move churches, finish campus, get jobs. Expect
   half of any cohort to vanish — recruit twice as many as you need.
4. **They will promise features you don't have.** Give them one sentence to memorise: *"Giving comes
   later this year — today it is members, attendance and SMS."*
5. **Do not let the training become a church service.** Two hours, timetabled, laptops open. If it
   turns into preaching, nobody activates.
